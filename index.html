Bio

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Power Engineering at the University of Calgary, affiliated with the DGRI Lab and the Grid Foresight Lab.

Prior to my Ph.D., I worked for nearly five years as a High Voltage Substation Design Engineer at Khorasan Power Engineering Consultant (Moniran) Company, designing over 20 high-voltage substations across eastern Iran. I hold an M.Sc. from Tarbiat Modares University and a B.Sc. from the University of Tehran, both in Electrical Power Engineering.

Research

My research focuses on transactive energy markets for net-zero electrical distribution systems. I develop data-driven frameworks for distribution locational marginal emission (DLME) estimation and emission-aware market clearing under congested network conditions.
