Bio

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Power Engineering at the University of Calgary, affiliated with the DGRI Lab and the Grid Foresight Lab. My research focuses on emission-aware transactive energy markets and distribution network optimization.

My background spans both industry and academia over nearly a decade. Prior to my Ph.D., I worked for nearly five years as a High Voltage Substation Design Engineer at Khorasan Power Engineering Consultant (Moniran) Company. I have hands-on experience in state estimation, power system protection, T&D operation, factory equipment testing, and on-site field inspection. I hold an M.Sc. from Tarbiat Modares University and a B.Sc. from the University of Tehran, both in Electrical Power Engineering.
